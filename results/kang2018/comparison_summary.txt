MECHANISTIC EXPLANATION — CellChat ZERO-FLOOR:
  4/5 capacity-only pairs have CellChat trimean = 0.0000
  for ALL cluster combinations tested.

  This is a STRUCTURAL property of the trimean formula:
    trimean(x) = 0.25*Q1 + 0.5*Q2 + 0.25*Q3  (Jin et al. 2021 eq. 1)
    => trimean = 0 whenever Q1 = Q2 = 0
    => Q1 = Q2 = 0 whenever fewer than 50% of cells express the gene
    => gene sparsity > 50% causes a hard zero regardless of true signal

  NEUROSCOPE computes per-donor cluster means (one value per donor),
  then estimates KSG MI on the 16 pseudobulk vectors.
  Inter-donor variation is retained even at 15% per-cell sparsity.
