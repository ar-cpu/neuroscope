8. METHODS NOTE (for paper writing)
===================================
Channel capacity estimation: KSG k-NN mutual information estimator
  (Kraskov, Stoegbauer & Grassberger 2004, Phys Rev E 69:066138)
  I(X;Y) = psi(k) + psi(n) - <psi(nx+1) + psi(ny+1)>
  Capacity = I(ligand ; response) / H(ligand)  [0..1]
  Permutation test: 199 shuffles of Y, p = fraction(null >= observed)

Bootstrap confidence intervals:
  500 resamples of the n paired donor observations with replacement.
  95% CI = [2.5th, 97.5th] percentile of bootstrap mi_norm distribution.
  Adaptive k used consistently across all bootstrap resamples.
  Wide CIs at n=149 reflect irreducible small-sample variance;
  relative channel ranking is valid even when individual CI is wide.

Differential testing: two-sample Wilcoxon rank-sum on per-cluster-pair
  strengths (strength test) and capacities (capacity test). BH-FDR.

Causal validation: instrumental variable / 2SLS regression.
  Instrument: donor_id (natural experiment, analogous to MR in GWAS).
  Weak instrument filter: first-stage F > 10.

NMF: non-negative matrix factorization of the [cluster-pair × L-R]
  communication tensor. Rank selected by elbow (<10% improvement).

Normalization: library-size median-ratio (scran-style), log1p.
HVG selection: degree-2 polynomial regression log(var)~log(mean);
  top 3000 genes by residual variance.
